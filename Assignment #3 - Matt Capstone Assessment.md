My senior design project is Realtorial, an augmented reality (AR)-based platform for creating
and following tutorials. Realtorial will guide users through tasks via a head mounted display
(HMD), providing guidance to users via an AR overlay that finds and highlights the object(s)
needed for the task and provides cues for what to do with the objects(s). From the tutorial
creation side, users will “record” themselves performing actions, making note to call out
necessary objects and their manipulations. From there, tutorial-specific machine learning (ML)
computer vision (CV) models will be trained. For tutorial followers, Realtorial will use the
models from the creators to segment the same objects within their space. Object manipulations
specified by the creators will also be shown, allowing tutorial followers to be guided through the
task in their own, likely different, physical spaces.

My motivation for choosing this project is because I have always been drawn to platforms like
YouTube for learning how to do things. Tutorials on this and other sites have guided me through
so many different things including installing mods for Minecraft, changing my car’s oil, and 3D
printing. In addition, virtual and augmented reality have always intrigued me as a way to
experience content on from the digital world, but it hasn’t seen the adoption the world may
have expected when devices first came out. As such, I thought combining these two things
would make for a cool experience for users, whether they need help building furniture or
changing a light switch. The fact that the product will adapt to the user’s environment to assist
them is something I have never seen before, and I’m excited to bring it to life. I’m also excited to
learn the ins and outs of using a game engine, as both co-op and school hasn’t required me to
do so yet.

I expect the resultant product from this project to be able to allow a user to create a simple
tutorial, and for a different user to be able to follow along, even with little to no experience in
the area covered by the tutorial. My preliminary approach to designing a solution involves
leaning on my experience with ML/CV, while researching methods for designing the UI and
communication elements with which I have less familiarity. I have downloaded Unity and began
experimenting with it, as this is my initial guess for the engine that will be most appropriate for
this project. To self-evaluate for this project, I think the best measure for me to use will be the
ease with which my team and my contributions can be integrated, as well as the reactions
produced by users (family, friends, etc.) of the system as we develop it. I can imagine in my mind
giving the product to someone else and them being wowed by how easily they are able to
accomplish something they’ve never done before and am holding myself to making that a
reality. I believe it is an ambitious project, however I believe my team and I will be able to scope
it such that we can produce a functional and impressive final product.
