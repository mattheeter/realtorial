# Realtorial Constraints Essay

The first hurdle Realtorial’s development faces are various economic constraints
including our reliance on freeware, necessity of purchasing a device, and the product’s
potential for revenue generation by corporations that choose to use it. Our project’s
limited budget necessitates relying on open-source software such as Unity and PyTorch,
and our chosen head-mounted display (HMD)’s software development kit (SDK). If a
corporation were to decide to use our product, there are tangible financial benefits that are
provided to them by their customers’ increased ease of doing whatever they offer tutorials
for.

Our next constraint will be the legal aspect of our product. The first legal aspect of
our product is that our device relies on input from various users. Users upload tutorials
they make to be housed in our software. This could lead to situations where users upload
tutorials on activities that could be seen as unsafe to the general public. Situations like this
could possibly make us liable for any harm caused. The second legal aspect is that Unity
and IDE have possible commercialization issues. Both Unity and the IDE we use for this
project contain agreements to not allow us to commercialize our product or have us fork
over a sum of our profits if we decide to release our product fully to the public as a finished
product.

The third constraint hinges on the security of the product’s users. Meta, the
company that manufactures and develops our chosen HMD, is incentivized to collect user
data to effectively target advertising, among other things. With the many data input
modalities, the HMD provides (cameras, microphones, text, etc.), we will be sure to inform
users about the potential privacy concerns and implement security best practices on any
data we collect in making the product work well.
Our fourth and final constraint is about how our product affects the world socially.
The major social aspect of our product is that it can find a good place in the public sector.
Things get busy in a variety of fields; our product could be used to ease the strain of training
individuals in doing specific actions or allow people to transfer important skills onto others
without the need to have them teach. This will help others become much more educated
as gaining knowledge will be more available and interactive for the public.
